Home health care and outpatient home-based physical therapy are both delivered in the home, but they differ in purpose, eligibility, and Medicare coverage. Home health care is designed for patients who are homebound and need skilled medical services under Medicare Part A. Outpatient home-based physical therapy is covered under Medicare Part B and focuses on improving mobility and independence without requiring homebound status.
