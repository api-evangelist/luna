Pickleball is highly addictive and incredibly fun, but the repetitive swinging and serving can take a heavy toll on your upper body. Whether you are dealing with a strained shoulder or "pickleball elbow," returning to the court requires a structured approach to prevent further damage. This injury recovery guide breaks down the five phases of recovery, showing how in-home physical therapy can help you restore mobility, regain strength, and safely return to play.
