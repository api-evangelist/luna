Movement and strength physical therapy focuses on improving how the body moves to build strength, reduce pain, and support long-term function. Rather than treating symptoms in isolation, it evaluates functional movement patterns, balance, coordination, and posture to develop personalized treatment plans. Care may include therapeutic exercise, movement retraining, and education to promote safer, more efficient movement.
