Recovering from a rotator cuff injury or surgery requires more than rebuilding shoulder strength. Successful recovery involves restoring mobility, retraining movement patterns, and protecting healing tissues throughout each phase of rehab. This guide breaks down five essential steps for safe, effective rotator cuff recovery with support from personalized, in-home physical therapy.
