In-home physical therapy for seniors provides a convenient, personalized way to improve mobility, reduce fall risk, and maintain independence without traveling to a clinic. Licensed physical therapists deliver one-on-one outpatient-level care directly in the home, tailoring treatment to real-life movement and daily routines. This approach supports seniors recovering from surgery, managing chronic conditions, or addressing balance concerns.
