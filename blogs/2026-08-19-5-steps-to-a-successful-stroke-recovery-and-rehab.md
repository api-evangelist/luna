Stroke recovery and rehab focuses on helping the brain and body reconnect through consistent, guided movement. This guide explains the five essential phases of recovery, including early mobilization, neuroplasticity, strength rebuilding, and functional movement training. In-home physical therapy supports recovery by helping patients practice real-life movements in a familiar environment.
