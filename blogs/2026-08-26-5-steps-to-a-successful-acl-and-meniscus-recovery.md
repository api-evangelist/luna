Recovering from a combined ACL and meniscus surgery requires patience, precision, and a carefully structured rehabilitation plan. Whether you were injured on the ski slopes or during a weekend game, healing involves protecting the joint, restoring full motion, and progressively rebuilding strength and neuromuscular control. This guide breaks down the essential phases of recovery and explains how outpatient physical therapy delivered in your home can help you safely return to your active lifestyle without the hassle of traveling to a clinic.
