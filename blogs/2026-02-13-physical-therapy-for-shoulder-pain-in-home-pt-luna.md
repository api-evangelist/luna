Shoulder pain can limit reaching, lifting, dressing, and sleeping comfortably. Physical therapy for shoulder pain focuses on improving joint mobility, building strength, and restoring functional movement. Treatment addresses common causes such as rotator cuff irritation, repetitive strain, and stiffness through guided exercise and hands-on care.
