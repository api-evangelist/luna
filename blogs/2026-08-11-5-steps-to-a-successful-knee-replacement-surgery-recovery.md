Recovering from a knee replacement requires a clear plan, early movement, and consistent physical therapy. This guide breaks down the five phases of a successful recovery, starting before surgery even begins. With the right preparation and support from Luna’s in-home physical therapy team, patients can manage pain, regain mobility, and return to everyday activities with greater confidence.
