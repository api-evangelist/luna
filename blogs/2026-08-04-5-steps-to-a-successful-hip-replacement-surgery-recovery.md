Recovering from hip replacement surgery takes consistent movement, guided rehab, and a clear recovery plan. This guide outlines the five essential phases of hip replacement recovery, from pre-surgical preparation to rebuilding strength and walking independently again. In-home physical therapy helps patients improve mobility, restore balance, and regain confidence safely in their everyday environment.
